Plate

Every panel is a leather plate with four engraved brass corners. Radius stays near zero — this world does not have rounded rectangles in it. Anything you would write on becomes vellum instead, which is why the character sheet is the only pale surface in the app.

Leather plate

Engraved corners, a diamond before the title, a hairline that fades at both ends.

Raised plate

Lighter leather, for anything floating over the table.

Selected plate

A brass edge and nothing else. Selection never floods a surface.
